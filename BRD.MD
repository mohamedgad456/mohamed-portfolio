# Business Requirements Document (BRD)

## 1. Project Overview

**Project Name:** Personal Portfolio Website  
**Project Type:** Personal Branding / Professional Portfolio  
**Owner:** Mohamed Hisham Gad

The goal is to create a personal portfolio website that presents Mohamed Hisham Gad’s education, experience, projects, technical skills, and career interests in a clear and professional way.

---

## 2. Business Objective

The website should:

- Build a strong professional online presence.
- Showcase technical skills, experience, and projects.
- Give recruiters and potential employers a quick overview of the background and capabilities.
- Make the CV and professional contact information easily accessible.

---

## 3. Digital Identity & Positioning

**Positioning:** Data Engineer · Data Analyst · AI Engineer

The website should reflect a modern and professional identity with a focus on:

- Data Engineering
- Data Analytics
- Artificial Intelligence
- Machine Learning
- Software Engineering

---

## 4. Target Audience

The main audience includes:

- Recruiters
- Hiring managers
- Potential employers
- Technical professionals
- Academic and professional contacts

---

## 5. User Needs

Visitors should be able to easily:

- Understand who Mohamed Hisham Gad is.
- Learn about his education and experience.
- Explore his projects and technical skills.
- Access his CV.
- Find his contact and professional links.

---

## 6. Website Goals

The website should:

- Introduce the professional background clearly.
- Showcase education and experience.
- Highlight projects and technical skills.
- Provide easy access to contact information.
- Maintain a consistent professional identity.
- Work well across desktop and mobile devices.

---

## 7. Content Requirements

### Hero Section

- Name
- Professional title
- Short introduction
- Call-to-action buttons
- Professional links

### About Section

- Education
- Major and minor
- Professional interests
- Thesis
- Short professional summary

### Experience Section

- Organization
- Position
- Dates
- Responsibilities
- Technologies used

### Projects Section

- Project name
- Short description
- Technologies used
- Main contribution

### Skills Section

- Programming
- Frameworks & Technologies
- Data & Databases
- Data Engineering & Analytics
- Machine Learning & AI
- Tools
- Languages

### Contact Section

- Email
- LinkedIn
- CV
- Other professional links

---

## 8. Design Requirements

The website should have:

- A modern and professional look.
- A dark technical style.
- Consistent colors and typography.
- Clear and simple navigation.
- A responsive layout.
- Smooth and intuitive interactions.

---

## 9. Technical Requirements

- Build the website using React.
- Use reusable components.
- Keep the project structure organized.
- Use Git and GitHub for version control.
- Make the website ready for deployment.
- Follow the development process defined in the PRD.

---

## 10. Success Criteria

The project should:

- Clearly represent the professional identity.
- Present the main education, experience, projects, and skills.
- Be easy to navigate and understand.
- Work properly on different screen sizes.
- Follow the requirements defined in the PRD.
- Maintain the project through GitHub.

---

## 11. Constraints

- The CV and provided professional information will be the main source of website content.
- The design should remain professional and career-focused.
- The project should be completed within the available time.
- Development should follow the agreed project methodology.
